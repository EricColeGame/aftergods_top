## Graphical Fidelity vs. Performance Modes Across Consoles

For console gamers, selecting the right display mode can dramatically alter the feel of divine combat. Action-heavy titles require split-second timing to dodge mythological area-of-effect spells and punish heavy attacks. Modern console builds of *Aftergods* bridge this gap by offering distinct operating profiles inside the settings menu.

### Fidelity Mode

Fidelity Mode prioritizes asset resolution, dense environmental shadows, volumetrics, and subtle ambient reflections. This preset is ideal for players who want to soak in the ruined divine architecture and detailed character models. The frame rate is capped at a steady 30 to 60 frames per second depending on console model, delivering crisp 4K presentation at the cost of rapid camera responsiveness.

### Performance Mode

Performance Mode drops baseline render resolution to dynamic intermediate targets while keeping high temporal upscaling active. In exchange, the engine targets a fluid 60 frames per second, which reduces input delay significantly. Player experience and community testing confirm that parry windows feel much more generous in Performance Mode due to reduced display latency.

### High Frame Rate (HFR / 120Hz) Mode

For displays that support HDMI 2.1 and variable refresh rate (VRR), High Frame Rate mode pushes the engine toward 120Hz output. While visual effects like volumetric fog and depth-of-field are scaled back, this preset delivers the lowest possible input lag, making it the definitive choice for hardcore raid trials and competitive survival modes.

## Handheld and Portable Gaming Tuning

The emergence of portable PC gaming has expanded the range of **Aftergods platforms**, allowing players to take divine hunts on the road. Running an action title on a battery-constrained device requires careful power management and in-game preset calibration.

When configuring settings for portable gaming systems like the Steam Deck or similar handheld devices, the goal is finding a stable balance between frame pacing and battery life. Setting the device display refresh to 40Hz with an in-game lock of 40 FPS provides noticeably smoother camera motion than 30 FPS, while drawing substantially less wattage than an uncapped 60 FPS profile.

| Handheld Profile | TDP Setting | Resolution Scale | In-Game Graphics Preset | Battery Life Estimate | Target Framerate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Power Saver** | 9W – 10W | 720p (FSR Performance) | Low Details, Shadows Off | ~2.5 – 3.0 Hours | 30 FPS Locked |
| **Golden 40Hz Balance** | 12W – 13W | 800p (FSR Balanced) | Medium Textures, Low Shadows | ~1.8 – 2.2 Hours | 40 FPS Locked |
| **Docked Performance** | 15W – 25W | 1080p (FSR Quality) | Medium Details across all | Plugged / Docked Mode | 45–60 FPS Dynamic |

### Recommended Handheld Tweaks

- **Cap Framerates System-Wide:** Avoid letting the GPU push unnecessary frames during menu navigation or cutscenes to keep thermal throttling minimal.
- **Enable Temporal Upscaling:** Utilizing FSR or XeSS at "Balanced" modes recovers substantial overhead without smearing fast-moving particle effects.
- **Adjust HUD Scaling:** Handheld screens can make complex UI readouts hard to track. Increase HUD scale to 110% in the gameplay accessibility settings for clear visibility during chaotic fights.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which of the Aftergods platforms offers the smoothest competitive frame rate?
High-end Windows PC hardware provides the highest ceiling for smooth frame rates, supporting refresh rates of 144Hz, 240Hz, and beyond with low input latency. However, for console enthusiasts, the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X offer dedicated 120Hz performance profiles when paired with a compatible HDMI 2.1 display.

### Can I transfer my progression between different Aftergods platforms?
Yes, cross-progression is supported across major systems through a centralized game account. By linking your console and PC platform accounts to the same service credentials, your unlocked gear, character levels, and campaign milestones sync seamlessly when switching devices.

### Is crossplay enabled by default across all supported hardware?
Crossplay is active out of the box for cooperative modes like Pantheon Raids and Story Co-op, pairing players regardless of their hardware ecosystem. In competitive or ranked survival modes, players have the option to toggle input-based matchmaking in the settings to ensure balanced competition.

### Does the game run well on portable handheld systems?
Community feedback confirms that the game runs comfortably on portable handhelds when visual settings are adjusted. Locking the refresh rate to 40Hz and using modern upscaling technologies like FSR provides a smooth, playable experience while preserving battery life.
