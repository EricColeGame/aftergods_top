## The Premise and Setting: How the Divine Descent Transformed Tokyo

The foundational catalyst of the story begins with the sudden arrival of massive, enigmatic deities known simply as "Gods." Rather than descending as benevolent protectors or classical mythological figures, these beings manifested with terrifying biological and metaphysical weight. Their mere presence warped local geography, emitting hazardous miasma and lethal phenomena that forced authorities to evacuate massive metropolitan sectors.

The primary staging ground for the narrative is Tokyo's cordoned Quarantine Zone, colloquially referred to as the "God Zone." Within these heavily monitored perimeter fences, ordinary civil laws cease to exist. Nature has been radically altered, filled with bizarre mutations, flesh-warping anomalies, and atmospheric conditions toxic to unprotected humans.

To manage this crisis, the surviving government established specialized institutions tasked with containing outbreaks, extracting anomalous resources, and monitoring divine movement. However, community reports and narrative clues quickly reveal that these institutional defenders harbor hidden agendas of their own, setting the stage for a layered conspiracy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the central premise of the Aftergods plot?
The **Aftergods plot** revolves around modern Tokyo following the sudden descent of titanic, incomprehensible divine beings. The story tracks Waka Kamikura, a young woman who infiltrates the quarantined "God Zone" to uncover the fate of her missing friend. After an encounter forces her into a parasitic bond with a serpent deity, she finds herself hunted by secretive research agencies while navigating terrifying cosmic threats.

### How does the power system work within the story?
Powers are not gained through formal training or spiritual cultivation. Instead, supernatural abilities stem from direct biological contamination or symbiosis with divine tissue. Utilizing these abilities damages the host's body and mind, meaning characters pay a severe psychological and physical cost every time they tap into divine power.

### Is the series primarily action-focused or psychological horror?
While the manga includes breathtaking, high-impact clashes between anomalous entities and military forces, it is primarily a psychological cosmic horror series. The narrative focuses heavily on trauma, existential isolation, institutional manipulation, and the grim reality of living in a world where humanity is utterly outmatched.

### How does the Aftergods plot treat its primary antagonist forces?
The narrative avoids simple villain archetypes. The invading gods act out of incomprehensible cosmic nature rather than calculated malice. Meanwhile, the human institutions attempting to study and fight them often commit horrifying ethical violations in the name of species survival, blurring the line between protector and oppressor.
