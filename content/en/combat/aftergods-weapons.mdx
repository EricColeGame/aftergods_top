## Advanced Combat Tips for Weapon Mastery

Equipping top-tier **Aftergods weapons** requires managing stat affinities and learning mechanical nuances that separate novice players from veterans. Apply these combat principles during challenging encounters:

*   **Cancel Long Recovery Animations:** Heavy weapons feature prolonged recovery frames after downward smashes. Cancel these dead frames immediately by executing a directional dodge roll or casting a low-cost off-hand spell.
*   **Monitor Stagger Thresholds:** When an enemy's posture bar flashes yellow, prioritize charged heavy attacks. Light attacks deal minimal poise damage and waste critical stagger windows.
*   **Balance Element Infusions:** Never pit elemental weapons against matching damage resistances. Keep a neutral physical option or a contrasting elemental weapon in your secondary slot for elemental-resistant bosses.
*   **Manage Stamina Reserves:** Never deplete your stamina bar completely. Keep at least 15% of your stamina available so you can dodge out of untelegraphable retaliatory strikes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Aftergods weapons are best for beginners?
The sword-and-board setups or balanced polearms like the Astral Piercer are ideal for beginners. They provide generous attack range, predictable combo strings, and reliable defensive spacing while you learn enemy attack patterns.

### Can I respec my stats if I want to switch Aftergods weapons?
Yes. Mid-game progression unlocks ritual respec shrines in the central sanctuary hub. By exchanging rare ritual tokens, you can redistribute your attribute points to support different **Aftergods weapons** and experimental hybrid builds.

### How do I unlock weapon weapon ascension?
Weapon ascension unlocks after defeating the gatekeeper bosses in the late-game chapters. Once unlocked, take your Tier 3 weapon and divine remnants to the master forge to complete ascension and unlock custom rune sockets.

### Does weapon weight affect dodge rolls in Aftergods?
Yes. Your equipped weapon weight contributes to your total equipment load. Staying below 50% capacity ensures light, high-invulnerability dodge rolls, while exceeding 70% results in a slow "fat roll" that leaves you vulnerable to rapid attacks.
