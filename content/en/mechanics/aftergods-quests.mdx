## Core Archetypes and Categories of Aftergods Quests

The quest structure in Aftergods divides missions into distinct categories, each tailored to different stages of character growth and operational tempo. Understanding how these assignments differ ensures you never waste precious playtime chasing low-yield objectives.

### Main Campaign (Pantheon Fall)
Main story assignments take players across chronological regional zones, unlocking world tiers, permanent movement abilities, and key hub vendors. These missions cannot be repeated on the same character, but they provide the highest baseline XP yields and permanent celestial passives upon completion.

### Daily and Weekly Divine Decrees
Decrees serve as the core resource loop for active players. Refreshing on a fixed server cycle, these tasks emphasize dynamic monster bounties, dungeon clearances, and zone-wide cleansing events. Player experience reports indicate that completing a full daily decree rotation provides enough sanctified dust to maintain continuous equipment refinement.

### Faction Allegiance Tasks
Faction hubs offer specialized tasks that tilt alignment towards specific remnant covenants. Advancing through these chains unlocks unique ability augmentations, specialized cosmetic seals, and vendor discounts on rare materials.

### Emergent World Anomalies
These are unscheduled, zone-wide challenges triggered when players eliminate elite sentinels or cleanse corrupted shrines. Anomalies feature ticking timers and reward anyone within the perimeter when the objective clears.

## Troubleshooting and Quest Progression Bottlenecks

Even seasoned explorers hit snags when advancing through complex storylines or dynamic event triggers. Knowing how the engine handles quest state flags helps resolve progression deadlocks without opening support tickets.

### Missing Objective Spawns
If an elite target fails to appear at a marked waypoint, check the zone's anomaly status. World events occurring in the same geographic cell can temporarily suppress standard mission spawns until the active anomaly concludes or despawns.

### Inventory Full Lockouts
High-tier reward caches cannot be delivered to an overfilled inventory. If a finished objective fails to register completion, clear at least four bag slots to receive milestone chests and advance dialogue flags.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Aftergods Quests

### How often do daily Aftergods quests reset across global servers?
Daily decrees and regional bounties reset every 24 hours at 00:00 UTC. Any active daily mission in your log that has not been turned in before this cutoff will roll over into the new cycle, potentially preventing you from claiming that day's fresh bounty allocation.

### Can I abandon Aftergods quests without losing regional reputation?
Yes. Abandoning non-story bounties or daily decrees incurs no reputation penalties. However, any partial progress on stepped objectives will reset if you decide to pick the contract back up at a later time.

### Why are some Aftergods quests locked behind specific gear thresholds?
High-threat assignments, such as Mythic and Primordial Incursions, enforce gear limits to ensure players have adequate defensive baseline stats to survive unavoidable environmental ticks and boss enrage mechanics.

### Do Aftergods quests share progress automatically across party members?
Yes. Slay counts, object interactions, and ritual activations count for all nearby party members. As long as teammates remain within the zone perimeter, progress updates concurrently for everyone involved.
