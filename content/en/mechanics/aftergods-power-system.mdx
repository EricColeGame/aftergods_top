## Foundational Architecture of the Aftergods Power System

The fundamental architecture of the **Aftergods power system** relies on a singular premise: true divinity cannot be generated by mortals; it can only be harvested, inherited, or stolen. When the elder pantheon collapsed, their fractured power dissolved into atmospheric essence known across factions as Deific Ichor. Mortals who possess physiological compatibility—often referred to as Vessels or Conduits—can assimilate this essence to manipulate reality on a localized scale.

Unlike standard mana or stamina pools, divine assimilation does not replenish naturally through rest alone. Power generation requires a continuous cycle of resonance, channeling, and biological filtration. If an attuned warrior channels more ichor than their physical vessels can stabilize, the energy rapidly destabilizes the physical form.

To categorize this power distribution, observers and researchers utilize a standardized five-tier hierarchy. Each tier represents not merely an increase in raw output, but a qualitative leap in how a fighter bends physical laws.

## The Price of Divinity: Corruption and Backlash Mechanics

A central pillar of the **Aftergods power system** is its strict risk-reward balance. Divine power was never engineered to inhabit fragile mortal biology. Every second an active vessel channels high-grade essence, an internal counter—known as Divine Taint or Corruption—accumulates within their organs and soul matrix.

When corruption surpasses critical containment limits, combatants suffer acute systemic failures. Rather than simple health depletion, corruption alters gameplay and narrative combat dynamics by introducing cognitive impairment, erratic ability activation, and physical deformation.

### Corruption Thresholds and Combat Penalties

| Corruption Range | Operational Status | Stat Modifications | Manifested Side Effects | Tactical Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0% – 25%** | Stable Harmony | None (Baseline performance) | Faint energetic glow, sharpened focus | Standard passive cooldowns |
| **26% – 50%** | Minor Strain | +10% Output, -15% Stamina Recovery | Muscle tremors, audio-visual distortion | Periodic conduit vent strikes |
| **51% – 75%** | Critical Resonance | +30% Output, -35% Physical Defense | Skin calcification, memory lapses, erratic targeting | Relic purging, stabilizing consumables |
| **76% – 90%** | God-Blighted | +60% Burst, -60% Max Health | Hallucinations, involuntary ability discharge | Emergency stasis, ally suppression |
| **91% – 100%** | Apotheosis Rupture | Infinite Output (Temporary) | Irreversible transformation, death or permanent entity loss | None (Absolute point of no return) |

To survive extended engagements, elite practitioners employ "venting cycles." By expelling concentrated divine waste into environmental structures or specialized focus implements, fighters can drop their corruption levels back into the manageable 26%–50% bracket without disengaging from battle.

## Tactical Execution: Optimizing Combat Rotations

Excelling under the rules of the **Aftergods power system** requires strict execution rather than reckless power displays. High-level engagements play out like high-speed games of chess, where victory belongs to the combatant who forces their opponent into premature corruption overload.

### Step-by-Step Engagement Protocol

1. **Initial Assessment and Resonance Matching:** Scan the opponent’s aura signature to identify their core affinity. Suppress your own resonance until the adversary commits to an opening stance, concealing your primary element.
2. **Neutral Game Poking and Heat Management:** Use low-cost physical strikes reinforced by Tier 1 energy coatings. Keep your internal corruption below the 25% threshold while forcing the enemy to expend high-tier defensive barriers.
3. **Triggering the Resonance Spike:** Once an opening presents itself, intentionally spike your conduit output to enter the 26%–50% bracket. Capitalize on the +10% output bonus to shatter their primary barrier or interrupt casting frames.
4. **Enforcing Domain or Relic Control:** If the opponent attempts to match your output, deploy a localized domain or activate a deific relic to skew environmental essence in your favor.
5. **Venting and Reset:** Conclude high-burst rotations immediately with a disciplined venting sequence to bleed off accumulated corruption before structural degradation sets in.

Fighters who ignore these rules and push blindly for maximum output invariably trigger an Apotheosis Rupture. While the resulting power surge is catastrophic, it guarantees mutual destruction—a strategy reserved exclusively for desperate final stands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the Aftergods power system distinct from traditional magic systems?
Unlike standard mana systems where magical reserves regenerate effortlessly, this framework treats divine power as a toxic, borrowed resource. Power output is intrinsically tied to a biological and psychological cost via the corruption mechanic, meaning every powerful technique brings the user closer to permanent physical mutation or total loss of self.

### How does divine corruption impact ability usage during combat?
As corruption accumulates past specific thresholds (such as 25%, 50%, and 75%), combatants gain higher raw damage output at the cost of severe debuffs. These penalties include reduced physical defense, severe sensory hallucinations, erratic ability targeting, and accelerated stamina depletion.

### Can a mortal combatant safely reach Tier 4 within the hierarchy?
Under normal physiological conditions, reaching Tier 4 without permanent consequences is practically impossible. Attaining this stage requires a mortal to surrender their baseline humanity, usually resulting in complete absorption by the lingering will of the fallen deity or immediate biological collapse.

### What is the most effective way to counter an Abyssal affinity user?
The most reliable counter to Abyssal conduits is aggressive Solar/Celestial zoning combined with continuous movement. Celestial radiance rapidly overwhelms void absorption zones, denying the Abyssal fighter the ability to siphon stamina or anchor their gravity-based control skills.
