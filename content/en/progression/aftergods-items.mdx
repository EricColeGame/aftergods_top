## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Aftergods items should beginners prioritize first?
Beginners should focus on items that enhance base survivability and movement speed. A strong defensive chest piece, such as the Gilded Sentinel Cuirass, paired with mobility-boosting boots, ensures smoother leveling and reduces fatal mistakes while learning telegraphed enemy attack patterns.

### Can lower-tier items be upgraded to end-game viability?
While basic Mortal and Heroic gear cannot cross the gap into end-game raids due to limited trait slots, select Ascended items with perfect stat rolls can remain competitive through mid-tier raids when fully refined and socketed with high-grade gems.

### How does the luck attribute impact farming for rare drops?
The luck attribute boosts the conversion rate of item rarity drops from slain enemies and reward chests. While it does not guarantee specific boss weapons, player experience demonstrates that maintaining moderate luck gear during dungeon speed-runs noticeably increases Demigod and Celestial material yields over extended sessions.

### What should players do with duplicate equipment drops?
Avoid selling duplicate high-tier gear to standard vendors for raw gold. Dismantling duplicates at crafting benches provides crucial salvage components like Celestial Ore and Divine Shards, which are mandatory for late-game weapon refinement and socket overcharging.
