## Overview of the Progression System and Unlock Pathways

Progression in *Aftergods* branches across multiple meta-systems rather than a single linear talent tree. Players collect remnants of divine energy—primarily Divine Favor, Celestial Shards, and Anima Embers—during each run. Returning to the Sanctum allows you to invest these resources into permanent enhancements, expanding your survival odds across subsequent attempts.

Community reports emphasize that spreading resources too thin across every branch slows down your early momentum. Instead, prioritizing specific progression milestones yields passive multipliers that accelerate later resource collection.

```
Sanctum Altar (Core Meta)
 ├── Class Shrines (New Combat Archetypes)
 ├── Reliquary Vault (Passive Relics & Artifacts)
 ├── Armory of the Fallen (Weapon Modifiers)
 └── Celestial Gate (Realm Modifiers & Difficulty Tiers)
```

Focusing on high-value **Aftergods unlocks** early provides passive health pools, extra dodge charges, and increased shard drop rates. Understanding the primary currencies and their specific destinations prevents wasted meta-currency investments.

1. **Target Resource Modifiers First**: Prioritize Sanctum nodes that boost Celestial Shard collection before investing in cosmetic or secondary damage perks.
2. **Commit to Specific Feat Runs**: When working toward unlock criteria—such as scoring backstabs or performing parries—ignore run completion speed. Focus purely on hitting the requirement, then bank your rewards.
3. **Equip Economy Relics Early**: Always carry relics like the *Alchemist's Crucible* into the first two biomes. Swap them for combat-heavy relics at mid-run shops once your wallet is full.
4. **Leverage Safe Reset Points**: If an unlock requires clearing early chambers under strict criteria, restart the run immediately if you fail the condition in room one rather than dragging out a compromised attempt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should be my highest priority among early Aftergods unlocks?
Your highest early priority should always be survivability and economy upgrades at the Sanctum Altar. Unlocking an additional revive through *Tears of the Fallen*, followed by passive maximum health and shard gain multipliers, will dramatically increase your success rate and speed up subsequent runs.

### How do I unlock alternate weapons for my characters?
Alternate weapons are obtained in the Armory of the Fallen by defeating biome bosses with specific weapon types or completing combat trials. Once unlocked, you can freely switch starting weapons in the Sanctum before starting a new run.

### Do Aftergods unlocks carry over between challenge seasons or difficulty resets?
Yes, all permanent meta-progression upgrades, unlocked classes, and relic pool additions remain permanently tied to your profile. Increasing the difficulty via the Celestial Gate or Void Rifts modifies enemy behavior and rewards, but it never resets your hard-earned inventory.

### Can I turn off certain relic unlocks if they dilute my loot pool?
In the advanced tier of the Reliquary Vault, you can unlock the "Relic Sealing" trait. This feature allows you to disable a limited number of unlocked relics, preventing them from appearing during active runs and refining your build consistency.
