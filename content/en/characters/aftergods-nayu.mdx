## Practical Tips and Advanced Combat Tactics

According to ongoing player experience and combat simulations, piloting Aftergods Nayu demands disciplined resource rotation and spatial management. Implementing subtle behavioral adjustments will drastically improve your win rate across all content tiers.

### Master Animation Canceling
Nayu's standard melee animations possess slight recovery frames upon completion. By canceling the final recovery frame with an evasive sidestep or tactical dash, you shave valuable milliseconds off your attack cycle, dramatically increasing sustained damage output.

### Maintain Line of Sight Control
Never engage an encounter directly through narrow corridors where enemy area attacks overlap. Instead, approach from lateral flanks. Forcing enemy formations to turn around splits their focus and creates safe attacking lanes for your ranged allies.

### Resource Conservation
Avoid burning all mobility charges simultaneously. Staggering your movement skills ensures that if an unexpected boss mechanic forces an emergency reposition, you maintain the resources necessary to survive without relying on external intervention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What role does Aftergods Nayu perform best in?
Aftergods Nayu performs best as a high-mobility skirmisher and backline assassin. Rather than standing toe-to-toe with heavy tanks, Nayu thrives when flanking high-value squishy targets, applying armor sunders, and executing weakened opponents before retreating to safe positions.

### Is Aftergods Nayu beginner-friendly or high skill ceiling?
While Nayu's basic abilities are straightforward to pick up, the character features a relatively high skill ceiling. Success requires precise timing, mastery of invulnerability and evasion frames, and strict positioning discipline to avoid being overwhelmed by enemy counterattacks.

### Which stats should I prioritize first on Nayu?
Focus primarily on Critical Strike Rating, Agility, and Armor Penetration. These three attributes maximize Nayu's burst-damage windows while keeping your skill cooldowns fluid and responsive throughout extended combat engagements.
