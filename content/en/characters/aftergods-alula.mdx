## Frequently Asked Questions About Aftergods Alula

### Is Aftergods Alula suitable for free-to-play players?
Yes, **Aftergods Alula** provides incredible value for free-to-play accounts because her fundamental kit offers innate crowd control and defensive utility. Even without premium endgame gear sets, she performs admirably in standard progression content using mid-tier craftable equipment.

### What is the most critical stat to focus on for Alula?
While raw attack is helpful, Critical Strike Rate and Energy Recharge are the most impactful attributes for Alula. Ensuring that she consistently lands critical hits while rapidly charging her ultimate ensures continuous combat uptime and superior damage output.

### Can Alula function as a solo carry?
While Alula deals high burst damage, she operates best as a hybrid carry paired with an energy generator or a dedicated frontline tank. Relying on her as a solitary frontline damage dealer can leave her exposed to aggressive boss mechanics during cooldown downtime.

### How do you cancel skill recovery animations with Alula?
You can cancel the recovery frames of her basic attacks and Celestial Rift by initiating a short directional dash or triggering Astral Aegis immediately as the hit connects. This technique shortens your rotation cycle and increases overall actions per minute.
