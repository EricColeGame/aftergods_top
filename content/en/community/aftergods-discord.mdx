## Server Rules, Etiquette, and Safety Practices

With thousands of players collaborating in a single digital space, community rules protect everyone from malicious links, unwanted harassment, and game-breaking exploits. Staying compliant ensures your account maintains clean standing.

### Essential Rules to Remember
- **Zero Tolerance for Phishing:** Never share third-party gold selling or account trading links.
- **Respect Spoilers:** Use Discord spoiler markdown (`||spoiler text||`) when analyzing hidden story elements or secret encounters.
- **Constructive Feedback:** Critique balance changes politely. Harassing developers or community managers results in immediate mutes or bans.
- **Keep Channels Focused:** Avoid trading talk in general chat or posting memes in technical bug-submission threads.

If you encounter suspicious users or receive unsolicited DMs offering cheats or promo codes, report them immediately through the support ticket tool within the **Aftergods discord**.
