## How to Verify Authentic Aftergods Merch and Avoid Bootlegs

Spotting legitimate Aftergods merch requires attention to fine detail. As demand surges between major content releases, third-party marketplaces often see an influx of low-resolution reprints and unauthorized cast molds. These replicas frequently use stolen community artwork or direct promotional asset rips that lack the sharp vector crispness of official prints.

Player experience and community reports highlight several telltale signs of bootleg goods. Counterfeit apparel often relies on cheap direct-to-garment (DTG) printing that feels sticky to the touch and cracks after a single cycle through a standard clothes dryer. Furthermore, fake acrylic standees frequently exhibit cloudy edges, misaligned offset printing, or scratched surfaces resulting from absent protective transit films.

To keep your spending safe, consult verified hubs and browse authorized distributions such as the [Steam community store](https://store.steampowered.com) or certified publisher storefronts before sending funds to unvetted third-party auction listings.

| Verification Checkpoint | Official Aftergods Merchandise | Common Counterfeit Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Packaging & Tags** | Branded tags, custom seal bags, barcode labels | Plain clear polybags, missing care tags |
| **Graphic Clarity** | Sharp gradients, crisp micro-details, vector line work | Pixelated edges, color banding, washed-out tones |
| **Fabric Quality** | Pre-shrunk cotton, substantial weight (240–400+ GSM) | Thin synthetic blends, irregular hems, chemical odor |
| **Hardware & Fasteners** | Custom branded pull-tabs, debossed snaps, YKK zippers | Unbranded generic plastic or flimsy zinc zippers |
| **Certificate of Authenticity** | Foil-stamped or numbered serial cards for premium drops | Missing paperwork or photocopied slips |

## Caring for Your Collection: Preservation, Display, and Maintenance

Preserving your Aftergods merch is just as vital as securing it during high-pressure storefront drops. Without appropriate maintenance, environmental factors like ultraviolet sunlight, humidity, and abrasive cleaning can degrade delicate pigment inks and brittle resins over time.

For fabric care, always invert graphic tees and hoodies before laundering. Cold water cycles reduce the mechanical friction that tears micro-inks away from fabric fibers. Never apply an iron directly to printed regions; if smoothing wrinkles is necessary, steam the fabric from the inside or lay a clean pressing cloth over the front.

Physical figures, acrylic standees, and desktop accessories benefit from dedicated dust-control routines. Acrylic items scratch easily when wiped with rough paper towels; use a clean, dry microfiber cloth intended for camera lenses instead. For those displaying resin statues, maintain a stable ambient temperature away from direct window sills to prevent delicate thin parts from warping under heat stress.

| Material Group | Primary Threat | Recommended Tool | Cleaning Frequency | Golden Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Printed Cotton** | Color fading, heat cracking | Mesh wash bag, cold water cycle | As needed after wear | Never tumble dry on high heat |
| **Cast Acrylic** | Surface scuffs, fingerprint haze | Optic microfiber cloth | Every 2–4 weeks | Never use alcohol or ammonia cleaners |
| **Resin Sculptures** | UV yellowing, dust accumulation | Soft goat-hair cosmetic brush | Once a month | Keep out of direct sunlight |
| **Paper Prints** | Paper yellowing, moisture ripples | Acid-free archival matting | Once at installation | Mount under UV-shielded acrylic |
| **Metal Pins** | Oxidation, needle bending | Anti-tarnish storage strips | Biannually | Store in climate-controlled pin books |

## Frequently Asked Questions About Aftergods Merch

### Where can I buy official Aftergods merch safely?
Always begin by checking official announcements posted on the creator's verified social channels, publisher portal, or partnered storefronts. For secondary trades, stick to reputable fan communities that enforce buyer protection and require verified timestamped photos, ensuring that every piece of Aftergods merch you receive is authentic.

### How can I tell if an Aftergods shirt or hoodie is authentic?
Look at the neck label, print method, and fabric density. Genuine garments typically feature custom woven or printed size labels, reinforced shoulder seams, and dense, durable cotton rather than generic, lightweight synthetic blends. The print should feel integrated with the weave rather than resting as a thick, plasticky film.

### What is the best way to clean an Aftergods desk mat?
Fill a basin with lukewarm water and a drop of mild dish detergent. Gently scrub the cloth surface using a soft sponge or micro-bristle brush, avoiding aggressive scrubbing along stitched edges. Rinse thoroughly with clean water, press flat between two dry towels to extract moisture, and allow it to air-dry flat away from direct heat.

### Are fan-made community creations considered Aftergods merch?
While fan-made creations are not official commercial merchandise, they form an essential and widely celebrated branch of community culture. Many collectors proudly display fan-crafted enamel pins, stickers, and art prints alongside official releases. Always ensure fan creators adhere to non-commercial community guidelines when commissioning or purchasing independent goods.
